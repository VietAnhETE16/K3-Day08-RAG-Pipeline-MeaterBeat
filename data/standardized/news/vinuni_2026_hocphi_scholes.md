In March 2019, Vingroup announced “The Science and Technology Scholarship Program for Overseas Study for Master’s and Doctoral Degree” initiative with a mission to build high quality human resources in the fields of science and technology for Vietnam. So far, 70 excellent students have been selected to pursue their graduate degree programs at 21 leading universities in the world.

We are pleased to share with you a brief video clip featuring the members of the Scholarship selection committee, representatives from foreign diplomatic missions in Vietnam, universities in the world and the Vingroup Scholars themselves and the journey of the Vingroup Scholarship Program thus far.

We invite you to watch and share this video clip so that more talented Vietnamese students can get access to the information and apply to the Vingroup Scholarship Program.

To learn more about the Vingroup Science and Technology Scholarship Program for Overseas Study for Master’s and Doctoral Degrees, please visit: https://scholarships.vinuni.edu.vn/
